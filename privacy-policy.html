Schema Atlas for Dynamics 365: Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Schema Atlas is a browser extension that helps people query and understand data in Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Dataverse environments. This policy explains what data the extension touches and what it does with it. In short: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Data the extension reads

  • Data from your Dynamics 365 environment: the schema (table and column definitions, display names and choice labels) and the records you choose to look at. These can include personal information stored in your CRM, such as names, email addresses and phone numbers.
  • Your Dynamics user details: your user ID, name, email address, security roles and teams in that environment, shown on the Environment page.
  • The address of the Dynamics page you are on, to know which environment and which open record to work with.

The extension reads this data with your own Dynamics sign-in, by sending requests from your open Dynamics 365 tab. It sees only what your security roles already allow you to see.

How the data is used

Only to show it to you inside the extension: to display records, build and run your queries, and explain the data model. Schema Atlas is read-only. It sends only GET requests and cannot create, change or delete data in your environment.

What is stored, and where

  • The downloaded schema and display names, cached in your browser (IndexedDB) so the extension starts faster next time.
  • Your recent queries, the environments you have used and any notes you write, kept in your browser's local storage.

All of this stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data in Chrome.

What is shared

Nothing. Schema Atlas does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no remote servers. Its only network requests go to the Dynamics 365 environment you are using.

Permissions

  • scripting: to run read-only requests inside your open Dynamics tab, so they use your existing sign-in, and to read which record is open.
  • unlimitedStorage: to cache the environment's schema, which is often 20–30 MB.
  • Access to https://*.dynamics.com/*: Dynamics 365 environments are hosted on these addresses.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information by Schema Atlas complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not sold, not transferred to third parties, not used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
